Flamengo pressed until the end, but ended in a draw against São Paulo at the Maracanã.

In their return to the Maracanã, Flamengo fought until the last moment, but drew 1-1 with São Paulo this Sunday (26), for the 20th round of the Brazilian Championship. Léo Pereira put the Mais Querido ahead at the beginning of the second half, but Calleri equalized for the visitors. In stoppage time, Samuel Lino even scored the winning goal for Flamengo. However, after VAR review, the play was invalidated due to an offside in the build-up. With the result, Flamengo reached 38 points in the national competition.

The match began with São Paulo having more possession in the attacking half. Gradually, Flamengo balanced the game, began to exchange passes with more quality, and started to get into the attack. The first big opportunity came in the 18th minute, when Samuel Lino found Erick Pulgar, who hit the crossbar.
Flamengo returned stronger for the second half and opened the scoring in the first few minutes. Lorran took a corner, Jorginho flicked it on at the near post, and Léo Pereira appeared in the six-yard box to subtly finish into the back of the net: 1-0.
